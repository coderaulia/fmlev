Native transfer and contract actions Back up first

Release a player as a free agent or move a contracted player to another club. The destination first team needs a spare slot. Each action asks for confirmation. Keep a backup save. Free-agent signing, loan recall, new loans and pending-transfer scheduling are unavailable.

Required for Move — target club * (pick it by name), yearly wage *, expiry year *. Optional — transfer fee. Terminate needs no fields.

Move uses the target club's first team and replaces the player's current wage and expiry. Enter the yearly wage in raw GBP. The game stores it as a weekly amount. A move fee debits the buying club's balance and credits the selling club's balance.
