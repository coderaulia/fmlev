Native transfer and contract actions Experimental

Release a player as a free agent, move a contracted player to another club, or sign a free agent (the destination first-team squad needs a spare slot). These redirect objects the game also references elsewhere: terminate, sign and move passed a screen check and a day tick on a throwaway save, but a save/reload was not confirmed. Use a throwaway save and Save As afterwards. Loan recall has no in-game test yet and stays disabled.

Sign and Move use the target club ID above (the club's first team). Wage (per year, raw GBP; stored per week as the yearly amount divided by 52) and expiry year are required to sign. A move fee is optional: it is taken from the buying club's balance and added to the selling club's (transfer budgets are not changed).
